About Lehner Analytics

International perspective. Project-led analysis.

An independent Berlin-based practice combining financial analysis, market research and technology understanding.

Experience

Work across markets, technologies and business models.

Lehner Analytics is shaped by analytical work across Germany, the United Kingdom and China, with projects spanning financial modelling, political economy, cybersecurity, digital identity, tokenisation and real-world assets.

The practice is built around questions that sit between disciplines: how market structure affects a business model, how operating assumptions translate into cash flow, and how technological or regulatory change alters risk.

Financial analysis

Models that connect operations, capital requirements and cash flows.

Market intelligence

Research that turns external developments into business implications.

Technology context

Analysis of non-standard and fast-evolving business models.

Working principle

Rigour matters most when the answer is not obvious.

The aim is to make assumptions explicit, uncertainty manageable and implications useful for the decision at hand.
