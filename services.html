Services

Focused analysis for consequential decisions.

Each engagement begins with the decision to be informed, the assumptions to be tested and the most useful form of output.

01

Market & Scenario Analysis

Assessment of market structure, demand drivers, competitive dynamics and external risks. Alternative scenarios clarify what could change, which assumptions matter most and how a decision performs across different conditions.

02

Financial Modelling & Stress Testing

Bottom-up financial and cash-flow models with transparent operating assumptions, sensitivities and downside cases. The objective is to expose dependencies and support capital allocation without unnecessary complexity.

03

Strategic Due Diligence

Independent analysis of a market, technology, counterparty or opportunity. Commercial, financial and technical perspectives are combined to test the central thesis and identify material gaps before commitment.

04

Monitoring & Executive Briefings

Ongoing tracking of decision-relevant indicators, translated into concise implications. Briefings focus on material changes, emerging risks and the questions leadership should revisit.

Engagements

Project-based or recurring.

Work can take the form of a focused analytical project, a concise decision memo or a recurring monitoring mandate.
