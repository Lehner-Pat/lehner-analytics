Services

Focused analysis for consequential decisions.

Support can be commissioned for a single question or established as a recurring analytical mandate. Every engagement starts with the decision to be informed—not with a standard report format.

01

Market & Scenario Analysis

Assessment of market structure, demand drivers, competitive dynamics and external risks. Alternative scenarios clarify what could change, which assumptions matter most and how the decision performs across different conditions.

Relevant for: market entry, business planning, investment assessment and strategic option reviews.

02

Financial Modelling & Stress Testing

Bottom-up financial and cash-flow models with transparent operating assumptions, sensitivities and downside cases. The work is designed to expose dependencies and support capital allocation rather than produce unnecessary complexity.

Relevant for: investments, new products, capital-intensive projects and business resilience reviews.

03

Strategic Due Diligence

Independent analysis of a market, technology, counterparty or opportunity. Research combines commercial, financial and technical perspectives to test the central thesis and identify material gaps before commitment.

Relevant for: acquisitions, partnerships, suppliers, technologies and private-market opportunities.

04

Monitoring & Executive Briefings

A tailored set of indicators is monitored and translated into concise implications for the organisation. Briefings focus on material changes, emerging risks and the questions leadership should revisit.

Relevant for: boards, finance functions, investors and teams operating in fast-changing markets.
