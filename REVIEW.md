# Lehner Analytics — neutral site review

## Positioning
The existing site has a credible core theme—independent analysis for high-stakes decisions—but the message is spread across several near-duplicate formulations. The revised version establishes one central proposition: **Volatile times require dynamic analyses.** It then explains the analytical method in three steps: structure uncertainty, test resilience and focus action.

## Structure
The previous services page presents six service categories, five possible formats and four client groups. This creates unnecessary choice and makes several offers appear interchangeable. The revised structure keeps three pages and consolidates the offer into four services that are familiar to established analytics and advisory buyers:

1. Market & Scenario Analysis
2. Financial Modelling & Stress Testing
3. Strategic Due Diligence
4. Monitoring & Executive Briefings

Engagement formats are reduced to three concise options. The separate client-category section is removed because the audience is already clear from the positioning and service descriptions.

## Copy
The revised copy removes phrases that weaken confidence, such as “developing analytical practice” and “initial areas of interest.” It avoids unsupported claims and uses direct, professional language. Each service explains the client question, the analytical work and the expected output without long bullet lists.

## Design
The fixed sidebar is replaced with a standard top navigation, giving the site a more established corporate and professional-services feel. The visual system uses a restrained navy, warm neutral background, clear hierarchy, strong spacing, limited card usage and responsive layouts. Decorative elements are subtle and implemented in CSS, keeping the site fast and low-maintenance.

## About page
The revised biography connects the international stations in Munich, London, China and Berlin to the current service offer. It accurately distinguishes completed degrees from additional Statistics & Data Science studies and shows how political economy, financial modelling, data methods and technology research combine in client work.
